COURSE GUIDE · 13.5 WEEKS

Data Science
for Life Insurance

From business requirements to an analysis portfolio: a project-based undergraduate course on understanding and anticipating life insurance policy lapse.

Work in teams of 3–5 members to investigate a fictional insurer’s retention problem, build reproducible analyses, and recommend evidence-based customer support. No prior insurance knowledge is assumed.

FROM EVIDENCE TO ACTION

The project

Which active policies should the insurer prioritize for retention support, when, and on what evidence?

The portfolio connects retention analysis, early warning exploration, and a simple lapse prediction model. You will define stakeholder requirements, prepare policy and payment histories, compare a business-rule baseline with a classifier, and explain results in business terms.

What you’ll deliver

  • Reproducible notebooks and a report or dashboard
  • A sample prioritization table
  • Execution and refresh instructions
  • A proposed pilot and monitoring plan
  • Individual reflections
